#recipe #cooking 

[Sally's Baking Addiction - My Favorite Cornbread](https://sallysbakingaddiction.com/my-favorite-cornbread/)

**Makes**: 1 × 20cm or 23cm square tin (or 23–25cm cast-iron skillet)

**Dry**
- 120g fine cornmeal
- 125g plain flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp baking soda
- ⅛ tsp salt

**Wet**
- 113g unsalted butter, melted and slightly cooled
- 67g brown sugar, packed
- 2 Tbsp (43g) honey

- 1 egg, room temp
- 240ml buttermilk, room temp (sub 2 tsp lemon juice / vinegar topped up to 240ml with whole milk, sit 5 mins)

**Optional Add-ins**
- 1–2 jalapeños, chopped
- 1 cup cheddar, grated
- ½ cup bacon, crumbled
- 1 cup blueberries
- ½ cup each dried cranberries + walnuts

**Method**

1. Oven 200°C, grease tin
2. Whisk dry together

3. Whisk butter, sugar and honey until smooth and thick
4. Whisk in egg, then buttermilk
5. Pour wet into dry, whisk just until combined, don't overmix

6. Bake until golden and toothpick comes out clean
	1. 23cm tin / skillet: ~20 mins
	2. 20cm tin: ~25 mins
7. Cool slightly before slicing
