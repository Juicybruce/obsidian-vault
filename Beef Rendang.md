#recipe #cooking 

[Andy Cooks - Beef Rendang](https://www.andy-cooks.com/blogs/recipes/beef-rendang)

**Serves**: 4

Serve with [[Roti Canai]] and rice

**Spice Paste**
- ~10 small dried chillies, deseeded
- 4 shallots
- 2 lemongrass, white part only
- 5 garlic cloves
- 5 candlenuts
- turmeric, thumb
- Ginger, thumb
- Galangal, thumb
- Salt
- Neutral oil

**Curry**
- Cinnamon stick
- 3 star anise
- 4 cloves
- 4 cardamom pods
- 4 kaffir lime leaves

- 1.2kg beef, 2cm cubes (slow cook cut, cheek, chuck, shin)
- 400ml coconut milk

**Finish With**
- 40g shredded coconut, toasted and pounded (kerisik)
- 1 Tbsp tamarind paste
- 1 Tbsp palm sugar

**Salad**
- Baby cucumbers, chopped
- Coriander
- Mint
- Lime
- Toasted peanuts

**Method**

1. Blitz paste with a splash of oil
2. Fry paste in remaining oil on med-high ~5 mins until fragrant
3. Add whole spices + lime leaves, 2 mins
4. Add beef, coat, then coconut milk
5. Barely simmer covered 2 hrs, then uncovered 30 mins, stir every 15

6. Meanwhile dry toast coconut until deep golden, pound to a paste
7. Stir in kerisik, tamarind and palm sugar, 5 mins

8. Toss salad with lime, top with peanuts, serve
