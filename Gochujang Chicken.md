#recipe #cooking 

[Little Spice Jar - Sticky Garlic Gochujang Chicken](https://www.littlespicejar.com/gochujang-chicken/)

**Serves**: 4

Serve with rice and steamed greens

**Velvet**
- 1 Tbsp water
- ¼ tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp sugar
- White pepper
- Salt
- Sesame oil
- 1 Tbsp soy sauce

- 570g chicken, 2cm pieces (thigh, breast, tenders)
- 3 Tbsp cornflour

**Sauce**
- 2 Tbsp soy sauce
- 60ml honey
- 2 Tbsp gochujang (more to taste)
- 1 Tbsp rice vinegar

- 5 garlic cloves, minced

**Finish With**
- Spring onion
- Toasted sesame seeds

**Method**

1. Mix velvet ingredients until baking soda dissolves
2. Toss chicken through, rest 10 mins
3. Stir in cornflour to coat

4. Whisk sauce
5. Fry chicken in 2 batches on med-high, undisturbed 2–3 mins, flip 2–4 mins until golden, set aside

6. Drop to med-low, cool pan 2 mins, fry garlic
7. Up to high, add sauce, bubble
8. Toss chicken back through, top with spring onion and sesame
