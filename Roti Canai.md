#recipe #cooking 

[How To Make Flaky Roti Canai - foodpassionical](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LZVMT8zNxjI)

**Makes**: ~10

Goes with [[Beef Rendang]] or similar

**Dough**
- 500g plain flour

**Wet Mixture**
- 190ml water
- 1 egg
- 4 Tbsp condensed milk
- 20g butter or margarine
- 1 Tbsp sugar
- ½ tsp salt

**Other**
- Margarine, for coating
- Oil, for stretching and cooking

**Method**

1. Mix wet, add to flour
2. Knead 5 mins (mixer) / 15 mins (hand), rest 15 mins, repeat x3
3. Divide into 10 (~100g), rub with margarine, cover and rest 8 hrs

4. Oil bench and dough, flatten with palm to ~18cm
5. Stretch (or toss) until paper thin, tears are fine
6. Drizzle oil, lift one edge and swirl into a coil, tuck end under, rest 10 mins
7. Flatten coil out to 15-19cm

8. Fry in oil on med 2 mins, flip 1 min until golden
9. Clap between palms to break up the layers
