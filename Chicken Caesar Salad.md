#recipe #cooking 

Croutons + dressing from [Serious Eats - The Best Caesar Salad](https://www.seriouseats.com/the-best-caesar-salad-recipe), chicken, bacon + eggs from [RecipeTin Eats - Chicken Caesar Salad](https://www.recipetineats.com/chicken-caesar-salad/)

**Serves**: 4

**Croutons**
- 45ml EVOO
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- Hearty bread, 2cm cubes
- Parmesan, finely grated
- Salt + pepper

**Dressing**
- 1 egg yolk
- 15ml lemon juice
- 2-6 anchovies, to taste
- 1 tsp Worcestershire
- Pressed garlic (from croutons)
- ~25g parmesan
- 80ml neutral oil (canola)

- 60ml EVOO

**Bacon + Chicken**
- 150g streaky bacon
- 500g chicken breast, pounded to ~1.2cm / butterflied
- Salt + pepper

**Salad**
- 2 heads cos/romaine, inner leaves only
- 2-4 eggs, soft boiled, halved
- Parmesan

**Method**

1. Oven 190°C
2. Whisk EVOO + garlic 30 secs, press through a fine sieve, keep the garlic for the dressing
3. Toss bread in garlic oil, then some parm, salt + pep
4. Bake ~15 mins until pale golden + crisp, toss with more parm, cool

5. Blitz yolk, lemon, anchovies, Worcestershire, pressed garlic and parm (stick blender / processor)
6. Stream in neutral oil while blending until emulsified
7. Whisk in EVOO by hand (not in the blender, goes bitter), season generously

8. Bacon into a cold pan, render on med-high until golden both sides, drain + chop
9. Season chicken, cook in bacon fat 5 mins / 2 mins, rest 5 mins covered, slice

10. Toss leaves with a few spoons of dressing, then half the parm + most of the croutons
11. Top with chicken, egg, bacon, remaining croutons + parm, extra dressing on the side
