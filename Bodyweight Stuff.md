# Calisthenics PPL Program

> Based on [this video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1mlN0yuxoLE)

**Rep target:** 5–15 reps per set is the sweet spot. When you can hit the top of a range with clean form, add reps — then when you can hit 15+ comfortably, move to the harder variation. Volume = sets × reps.

**Progressive overload:** Add reps → add a set → move to the next variation.

## Day 1 — Push

### Strength Ramp-Up

Start easy and build into working weight. These sets don't count toward your volume — they're just getting the joints ready.

- [Push-Ups](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1mlN0yuxoLE&t=163s) — 2 × 5 (easy, focus on form — [form guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1mlN0yuxoLE&t=179s))

### Working Sets

|Exercise|Sets|Reps|Notes|
|---|---|---|---|
|[Push-Ups](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1mlN0yuxoLE&t=163s)|3|8–12|Main horizontal push compound|
|[Dips](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1mlN0yuxoLE&t=295s)|3|6–10|Main tricep/chest compound|
|[Pike Push-Ups](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1mlN0yuxoLE&t=346s)|3|8–12|Overhead/shoulder push|

> **Progression:** Push-ups → when you hit 3×12 with solid form, add a set or begin working toward a harder variation (e.g. weighted or archer push-ups).

### Core

|Exercise|Sets|Reps/Duration|Notes|
|---|---|---|---|
|[Hollow Body Hold](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1mlN0yuxoLE&t=582s)|3|20–30 sec|Core tension fundamentals|
|[Superman](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1mlN0yuxoLE&t=596s)|3|10–15 reps|Lower back counterbalance|

### Cool-Down

Light stretching — chest, shoulders, triceps. 30–60 sec per stretch.

## Day 2 — Pull

### Strength Ramp-Up

- [Rows / Australian Pull-Up](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1mlN0yuxoLE&t=411s) — 2 × 5 (easy angle, long rest)

### Working Sets

|Exercise|Sets|Reps|Notes|
|---|---|---|---|
|[Pull-Ups](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1mlN0yuxoLE&t=428s)|3|5–8|If below 5 clean reps, sub rows|
|[Rows / Australian Pull-Up](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1mlN0yuxoLE&t=411s)|3|8–12|Horizontal pull compound|

> **Progression ladder:** [Assisted Rows](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1mlN0yuxoLE&t=387s) → Rows → Pull-Ups. Stay on rows until you can do 3×12 with a flat body angle, then move to pull-ups.

### Core

|Exercise|Sets|Reps/Duration|Notes|
|---|---|---|---|
|[Knee Raises](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1mlN0yuxoLE&t=603s)|3|10–15 reps|Hip flexor and lower abs|
|[L-Sit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1mlN0yuxoLE&t=614s)|3|5–15 sec hold|Work up from tucked version|

### Cool-Down

Light stretching — lats, biceps, rear shoulders. 30–60 sec per stretch.

## Day 3 — Legs

### Strength Ramp-Up

- [Air Squat](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1mlN0yuxoLE&t=685s) — 2 × 10 (slow and controlled)

### Working Sets

Pick your current level on the squat progression and work within it. Move to the next variation when you can hit the top of the rep range with clean form.

**Squat Progression Ladder:**

1. [Air Squat](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1mlN0yuxoLE&t=685s) _(start here)_
2. [Knee Press / Sissy Squats](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1mlN0yuxoLE&t=694s) — [variation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1mlN0yuxoLE&t=714s)
3. [Pistol Squat](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1mlN0yuxoLE&t=729s) / [Shrimp Squat](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1mlN0yuxoLE&t=721s) _(advanced)_

|Exercise|Sets|Reps|Notes|
|---|---|---|---|
|Squat (your current level)|3–4|8–12 each side|Use progression ladder above|

### Core

|Exercise|Sets|Reps/Duration|Notes|
|---|---|---|---|
|[Hollow Body Hold](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1mlN0yuxoLE&t=582s)|3|20–30 sec||
|[Knee Raises](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1mlN0yuxoLE&t=603s)|3|10–15 reps||

### Cool-Down

Light stretching — quads, hamstrings, hip flexors. 30–60 sec per stretch.

## Programming Notes

||Detail|
|---|---|
|**Frequency**|3 days/week with rest days between (e.g. Mon/Wed/Fri)|
|**Rest between sets**|90 sec–3 min for compounds; 60–90 sec for core|
|**Rep target zone**|5–15 reps — if below 5, exercise is too hard; above 15+, too easy|
|**Progress by**|Adding reps → adding a set → upgrading to a harder variation|
|**Test rep maxes**|Occasionally (every few weeks) — not every session|
