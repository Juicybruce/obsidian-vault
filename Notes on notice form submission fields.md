_Request a Notice to Mariners — form summary_

  

A new form has been built from the client's field list. Below is a plain-English

list of where we departed from that list, and how the form feeds the notice.

  

_Departures from the requested field list_

  

- **Section 2 (Notice details):** built as a **single-choice dropdown** (the list

suggested tick-all-that-apply). It also **does not tag the notice** — the choice

is recorded with the submission for reference only, because there is no matching

category on notices.

- **Section 3 (Latitude/Longitude & Nearest landmark or port):** these are recorded

with the submission, and an administrator copies the coordinates into the notice's

map when publishing (the form can't populate the map directly). The

`DD° MM.MMM'` format you asked for has been kept.

- **Section 5 (Start/End times & "Ongoing"):** captured and saved to the notice, but

**not yet displayed on the website** — showing them is a follow-up task.

- **Attachments vs image:** split into two fields — a **Featured image** (JPG/PNG)

and a separate **Attachments** upload for plans/maps/diagrams (PDF/JPG/PNG).

- **Not in your list, kept for staff:** **Notice type** (Notice/Advice) and

**Region** are set by staff, not the public. Region has been removed from the

public form and is now admin-only.

  

## How the form feeds the notice

  

- **Title is automatic:** each submission is given the next sequential code

(e.g. `M001-26`); staff simply add the rest of the title afterwards.

- **Notices arrive as "Pending"** for staff to review before publishing.

- **Saved onto the notice automatically:** description, waterway/area (location),

start & end dates, start & end times, the "ongoing" flag, featured image,

attachments, and the Notice/Advice type and Temporary/Permanent state.

- **Not saved onto the notice:** the submitter's name, organisation, phone and

email — these appear only in the form entry and the notification email to staff.
